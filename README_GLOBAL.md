# Catholic Network Tools — Global Edition 🌍🙏

**Community coordination platform covering every dimension of parish life: spiritual formation, material stewardship, communal care, and justice witness.**

**Built on:** GitHub (data) + Streamlit (UI) + Python (logic)  
**Deployed:** Free on Streamlit Cloud  
**Owned:** Community  
**Cost:** $0 (Free forever)

---

## **The Problem We Solve**

The Catholic Church touches people's lives in **7 interconnected dimensions:**

| Dimension | What It Covers | Current Tools |
|-----------|---|---|
| **🛐 Spiritual** | Sacraments, prayer, catechesis, reconciliation | Paper registers + WhatsApp groups |
| **🍞 Material** | Food pantry, homeless shelter, healthcare, tutoring | Excel + tribal knowledge |
| **👥 Community** | Pastoral care, grief support, RCIA, mentorship | Manual visits + memory |
| **⚖️ Justice** | Advocacy, migrant aid, prison ministry, living wage | Email threads + disconnected efforts |
| **👦 Formation** | Youth groups, young adults, families, seniors | Printed schedules |
| **💰 Stewardship** | Giving, budgets, volunteer hours, resource allocation | Closed spreadsheets + distrust |
| **📋 Admin** | Mass schedules, contact management, announcements | Multiple systems + chaos |

**Current State:** These exist in silos. A parishioner can't see how their donation supports the food pantry. Youth groups don't coordinate with social justice work. Pastoral care teams duplicate effort.

**Our Solution:** One platform. All dimensions visible. Community transparent.

---

## **What Catholic Network Tools Does**

### **For Parishioners (Anyone)**
- 🙏 **Prayer & Formation:** Join prayer circles, track prayer intentions, register for catechesis
- 🤝 **Community:** Sign up to visit homebound, bring meals to families in crisis, mentor youth
- 🍎 **Serve:** Volunteer at food pantry, tutor kids, visit prisoners, march for justice
- 💚 **Give:** Make donations (with full transparency on impact), track giving history
- 📱 **Know:** See Mass schedule, find groups, get pastoral announcements

### **For Pastoral Teams (Priests, DRE, Pastoral Assoc)**
- 📊 **Sacraments Dashboard:** Baptisms, first communions, marriages, funerals—with family histories
- 👣 **Pastoral Care Tracking:** Who's homebound? Who's grieving? Who's been visited this month?
- 🔔 **Outreach:** Automated reminders for birthdays, anniversaries, sacrament prep
- 📞 **Communication:** Bulk SMS, email, WhatsApp to specific groups
- 🎓 **Formation:** Track catechesis attendance, RCIA progress, youth group engagement

### **For Justice Teams**
- ✊ **Campaign Coordination:** Track advocacy efforts, letter-writing campaigns, protest attendance
- 🤲 **Community Organizing:** Map needs (homeless, food insecurity, education), coordinate response
- 📈 **Impact Reporting:** Show bishop/diocese what justice work accomplished
- 🌍 **Solidarity:** Connect Consolata Shrine (Nairobi) with All Saints (Virginia) on same campaign

### **For Finance Councils**
- 💵 **Full Transparency:** Every donation visible (anonymously if desired), spending justified
- 📈 **Impact Dashboard:** Show how stewardship supports spiritual + material + justice work
- 🎯 **Budget Alignment:** Link giving to strategic priorities (e.g., 20% to food pantry, 30% to formation)
- 🔍 **Audit Trail:** Every transaction logged; no surprises

### **For Bishops & Dioceses**
- 🏛️ **Aggregate Reporting:** See what 50+ parishes are doing in spiritual, material, justice dimensions
- 🌱 **Best Practices:** Learn what parishes are doing well; share across diocese
- 💡 **Innovation:** Identify emerging needs; fund new initiatives
- 🌍 **Global Network:** Connect dioceses across continents on shared campaigns

### **Data Flow**

```
Parishioner enters data in Streamlit app
        ↓
Data validated + cleaned (Python)
        ↓
Saved to CSV/JSON file
        ↓
Commit to GitHub branch
        ↓
Parish coordinator reviews
        ↓
Merge to main (data persisted)
        ↓
Streamlit refreshes
        ↓
Dashboard shows live update
```

**All data is:**
- ✅ On GitHub (searchable, forked, audited)
- ✅ In open formats (CSV, JSON)
- ✅ Version-controlled (full history)
- ✅ Community-readable (transparency)
- ✅ Forkable (every parish can have own version with own data)

## **The 7 Dimensions Explained**

### **1. 🛐 Spiritual (Sacraments & Prayer)**

**What it tracks:**
- Baptisms (families, dates, godparents, follow-up formation)
- First Communions (catechesis attendance, readiness assessment)
- Confirmations (sponsor assignments, retreat attendance)
- Marriages (premarital counseling completion, anniversary dates)
- Holy Orders & Religious Profession (vocational discernment tracking)
- Funerals (family support, grief group enrollment)
- Prayer circles (intention requests, outcomes, community bonding)
- Reconciliation (frequency, themes for spiritual direction)

**Why it matters:** The Church's highest calling is **sacramental life**. Tracking ensures:
- No one misses sacraments they're ready for
- Pastoral follow-up after key moments
- Families feel personally known
- Prayer requests receive communal support

### **2. 🍞 Material (Food, Shelter, Healthcare, Education)**

**What it tracks:**
- Food pantry (beneficiaries, dietary needs, frequency, donated items)
- Homeless shelter (beds available, residents, case management)
- Medical clinic (services offered, patients served, volunteer doctors)
- Tutoring program (students, subjects, progress tracking, success rates)
- Emergency assistance (rent, utilities, medical bills covered)
- Daycare (enrollment, subsidized spots, safety compliance)

**Why it matters:** **"Whatever you do to the least of these..."** (Matthew 25). Tracking ensures:
- Resources reach those most in need (not based on connections)
- No one falls through cracks
- Dignity preserved (personalized approach, not charity-as-transaction)
- Impact measured (show donors what their gifts accomplish)

### **3. 👥 Community (Pastoral Care & Relationship)**

**What it tracks:**
- Homebound visits (Who? When? What was shared? Follow-up needed?)
- Grief support (Death, job loss, divorce—who needs sustained care?)
- Conflict resolution (Mediation offered; relationships restored)
- Marriage prep (Couples tracked; success rate measured)
- Baptismal sponsor formation (Teaching godparent role)
- New parishioner integration (Welcome, connection, small group assignment)
- Youth mentorship (Teens paired with adults; trust-building milestones)

**Why it matters:** The Church is a **body**, not a building. Tracking ensures:
- People aren't isolated
- Crises are noticed + addressed
- New members feel wanted
- Trust is actively built

### **4. ⚖️ Justice (Advocacy & Community Organizing)**

**What it tracks:**
- Active campaigns (Living wage, refugee rights, environment, housing)
- Participation (Who's involved? Letters written? Actions taken?)
- Impact (Did the company raise wages? Did the law change? How many benefited?)
- Coalition partners (Other churches, NGOs, secular groups working together)
- Educational events (Learn-ins, talks, screenings about justice)
- Voting blocs (Coordinate Catholic voters for justice-aligned candidates)

**Why it matters:** **"Seek justice, love mercy, walk humbly..."** (Micah 6:8). Tracking ensures:
- Justice isn't vague ("we care about poor people")—it's **concrete** (this campaign won 2,500 workers a raise)
- Parishes amplify power through coordination
- Faith + action are linked
- Impact is measured + celebrated

### **5. 👦 Formation (Education & Spiritual Growth)**

**What it tracks:**
- Catechesis (Age groups, curriculum, attendance, comprehension checks)
- RCIA (Inquirers, catechumens, candidates—pace of formation tracked)
- Youth groups (Middle school, high school, young adults—activities, attendance, leadership)
- Adult Bible study (Groups, topics, engagement levels)
- Marriages ministry (Sponsor couples, enrichment events, divorce recovery)
- Senior formation (Spiritual direction, legacy projects, mentoring youth)

**Why it matters:** **"Teach them to observe all that I commanded..."** (Matthew 28:20). Tracking ensures:
- Formation is intentional (not ad hoc)
- People progress at right pace (not rushed, not stalled)
- Mentorship is structured (pairing, accountability)
- Generational knowledge transfers (elders mentoring youth)

### **6. 💰 Stewardship (Giving & Transparency)**

**What it tracks:**
- Individual giving (Donations, frequency, interests—e.g., "wants to fund justice work")
- Pledge drives (Goal, progress, incentives)
- Budget allocation (What % to sacramentals, formation, justice, material aid?)
- Grant writing (Diocesan grants, external funding for initiatives)
- Volunteer hours (Valued in monetary terms; shows total community investment)
- Asset management (Building, vehicles, shared spaces—maintenance, depreciation)

**Why it matters:** **Money is a spiritual issue.** Tracking ensures:
- Trust (Transparency eliminates accusations of misuse)
- Alignment (Giving supports stated priorities)
- Generosity (When people see impact, they give more)
- Sustainability (Can we fund this program long-term?)

### **7. 📋 Admin (Coordination & Communication)**

**What it tracks:**
- Mass schedules (Priests, deacons, readers, altar servers—assignments)
- Facility usage (Halls, parking, kitchen—who booked when?)
- Contact management (Phone tree, email lists, emergency contacts)
- Volunteer hours (For background checks, gratitude, event logistics)
- Document management (Policies, procedures, training records)
- Insurance & compliance (SafeParish requirements, CPR certifications)

**Why it matters:** **"Let all things be done decently and in order..."** (1 Cor. 14:40). Tracking ensures:
- Smooth operations (no double-bookings, missed Masses)
- Safety (background checks done, allergies noted, emergency plan updated)
- Continuity (new pastor doesn't lose institutional knowledge)

## **Quick Start**

### **For a Parishioner (5 min)**

1. Go to: https://catholic-network-tools.streamlit.app/
2. Click "Login with GitHub" (creates free account if needed)
3. Select your parish from dropdown (or add your own)
4. Fill profile: Name, prayer intentions, volunteer interests
5. **Done.** You're now visible + reachable to pastoral care + volunteers

### **For a Parish (15 min)**

1. Fork this repository on GitHub
2. Copy `parishes/template/` → `parishes/your-parish-name/`
3. Edit `settings.json` with parish info (name, location, priest, contact)
4. Deploy on Streamlit Cloud (free, takes 3 min: https://streamlit.io/cloud)
5. Share link in bulletin + WhatsApp

Your parish now has:
- ✅ Sacrament tracker
- ✅ Pastoral care dashboard
- ✅ Volunteer signup
- ✅ Giving + impact dashboard
- ✅ Prayer circle
- ✅ Formation tracking
- ✅ Justice campaign coordinator
- ✅ Mass schedule + announcements

### **For a Diocese (30 min)**

1. Fork repo
2. Deploy single instance covering all parishes
3. All 35 parishes feed into one dashboard
4. Bishop sees: attendance, sacraments, justice work, giving, formation engagement
5. Identify best practices, share across diocese
